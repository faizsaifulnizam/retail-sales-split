- **Observed-industry contributions over time** — `contrib_prices_pp` is a fixed-2025-weight calculation on the linked current-price indices, not an exact reconstruction of historical contributions. - **Coverage and residual** — use [`../outputs/contribution_reconciliation.csv`](../outputs/contribution_reconciliation.csv) alongside the extract. Schema: `month,series_group,covered_industries,covered_weight_pct,total_yoy_pct,covered_contrib_prices_pp,residual_prices_pp`. Show residual separately (or as an explicitly labelled residual series), with the published total as a reference. July 2026 retail residual is +0.0174 pp; June is +0.7088 pp. Do not label residual as the missing industries' measured contribution.
- **Filter** — industry picker; retail and F&B separated by `series_group`, never combined into one total. Exclude Total and excl-MV aggregate rows from industry contribution stacks.

**Data:** [`../outputs/tableau_extract.csv`](../outputs/tableau_extract.csv) — monthly, 2016 → latest, one row per industry × month: index levels (volume/value, original + SA), YoY %, and calculated value contribution in **pp of total YoY growth**. Regenerated by `python src/analysis.py`; null contributions remain unavailable, not zero. Growth contributions are not index-point changes.

A re-pull updates calculations, not the dated release transcription, explanatory prose or dashboard labels automatically. Follow the README refresh checklist and inspect coverage/residuals after every refresh. Historical estimates use current weights on linked series and do not recover contemporaneous old-base contributions.
